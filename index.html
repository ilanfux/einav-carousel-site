Einav Carousel

Einav Carousel is a private tool that helps one Instagram creator prepare carousel posts. It drafts texts, lays out slides and packages finished posts for manual upload. It runs on the owner's own computer and is not offered to the public.

How it uses Google Drive

With the creator's permission, the tool saves drafts, backups and finished carousel packages to folders it creates in her Google Drive, so she can open them on her phone. It asks only for the drive.file permission, which gives access to the files and folders the tool itself creates. It cannot see or change anything else in her Drive.
