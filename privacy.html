Privacy policy

Einav Carousel · Effective 14 November 2026 · Home

Who uses this app

Einav Carousel is a private tool used by a single Instagram creator and the person who maintains it for her. It runs on the maintainer's own computer. It has no public sign-up and no other users.

Google user data the app accesses

The app requests one Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This lets it create folders and files in the creator's Google Drive, and read or update only those files it created. It cannot see, read or change any other file in her Drive.

How the data is used

The app saves carousel drafts, draft backups and finished carousel packages (slide images, captions and hashtags) to its own folders in her Drive, so she can open them on her phone and upload posts herself.

Storage and sharing

Data stays in two places: the creator's own Google Drive and the maintainer's own computer. Data obtained through Google APIs is not sold, not shared with or transferred to any third party, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

Einav Carousel's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Access can be withdrawn at any time in the Google Account under Third-party apps and services. Deleting the app's folders in Google Drive deletes the data stored there.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the support address shown on the Google consent screen of Einav Carousel.
